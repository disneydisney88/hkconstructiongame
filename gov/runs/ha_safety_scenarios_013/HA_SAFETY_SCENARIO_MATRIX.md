# HA safety scenario matrix

Source: [HA Site Safety Handbook 2017](https://www.housingauthority.gov.hk/mini-site/site-safety/common/resources/handbook/201603/HB_res_tcen.pdf). PDF page numbers, not printed numbering. Independent game-design paraphrases; not official training/certification or latest law. Seven P1 priorities selected; only S07 implemented.
